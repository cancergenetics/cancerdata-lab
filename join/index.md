## PhD position — Paralogs in disease

We currently have funding for a PhD student to join the lab. The studentship covers EU tuition fees and a tax-free stipend of €22,000 per year. Due to the funding source, this position is only available to students from [the EU/EEA/UK/Switzerland](https://www.ucd.ie/students/fees/eufeeassessment/). This is a fully dry-lab role, and we are especially interested in hearing from applicants with a strong background in machine learning, genetics, or some combination of the two.

**Project overview.** Understanding why the mutation of some genes causes phenotypic defects while others are well tolerated is crucial both for understanding genetic variation in human populations and for developing new therapeutic approaches for inherited disease. Particularly important in this context are gene duplicates (paralogs). Because of their shared origin, pairs of paralogs often perform similar functions and may compensate for each other's loss. Yet 80% of Mendelian disease genes have identifiable paralogs, suggesting that the loss of these genes cannot be adequately compensated. The goal of the PhD is to understand why certain paralogs cause disease when mutated, and more generally how paralogs contribute to human genetic robustness. We are particularly interested in integrative and systems approaches.

In recent work we have explored how paralogs influence the response of cancer cells to genetic perturbation [(1)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1008466) and how they shape the evolution of tumour genomes [(2)](https://doi.org/10.15252/msb.202311987). Using proteomic approaches, we have also explored how mutation or deletion of one paralog can alter the abundance of another [(3)](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.09.26.615228). This project will move beyond cancer to look more generally at the role of paralogs in health and disease.

**Qualifications.** Applicants should typically have a primary degree in genetics, bioinformatics, or computer science. If you are not in this category, feel free to email to discuss. Expertise in any of the following would be a plus:

- machine learning
- network biology
- systems biology
- analysing large-scale genetics datasets
- evolutionary biology

**Application.** Please email [Colm](mailto:colm.ryan@ucd.ie) with a CV and cover letter. Informal enquiries are also welcome.
